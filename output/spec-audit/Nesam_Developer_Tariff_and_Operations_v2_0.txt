Extra distance must not duplicate the quoted base
Example: a Sedan is quoted for 300 km. Customer base = 300 × ₹15 + ₹400 = ₹4,900. Verified final
distance is 310 km. Add only 10 × ₹15 = ₹150; final base = ₹5,050. Partner base on the same billable

server derives the additional amount.

approved refund / amendment rule applies.
• Recommended: payout follows the booked duty category. A partner voluntarily using a larger eligible
car does not automatically earn a higher category rate.

requires explicit disclosure and approval.

Four-hour payout requirement
For completed trips where company holds money due to the partner, create a payout due time of trip
completed_at + 4 hours. Admin must be able to release the verified partner amount by bank/UPI or
another approved payout channel.
• States: Due, Processing, Paid, Failed and On Hold. A Paid state requires transfer confirmation/reference.
• Validate beneficiary and confirmed customer receipts. If a hold is required, record reason, owner and
resolution deadline; retain the original overdue timer.
• Escalate due/failed/overdue payouts. Retry safely without paying twice. Record initiated time and
confirmed transfer time separately.
• Email is a notification of payment status, not a payment mechanism. Actual bank credit timing must be
distinguished from transfer initiation.
Invoice and reconciliation
Invoice lines should identify service, dates/days, included and extra KM/time, bata, approved extras, tax,
total, paid amount and outstanding balance. Keep partner payout/margin lines out of the customer
invoice.
Earlier discussion mentioned wallet credits for amounts below ₹300. Its interaction with the four-hour payout
rule is not final. Define the threshold, withdrawal availability and priority before automating it; a wallet credit
must not disappear or become company revenue.
