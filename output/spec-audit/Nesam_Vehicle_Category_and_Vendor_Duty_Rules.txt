YES = category match, subject to approval, availability and capacity. NO = no automatic match. "Other SUV" excludes Innova and
Innova Crysta. Seating figures refer to the Admin-verified vehicle configuration.
Capacity is a separate requirement
6+1 = 6 passengers + driver; 7+1 = 7 passengers + driver. Store passenger capacity separately from the
model. SUV 7+1 requires at least seven approved passenger seats even if fewer passengers are travelling. A 6+1
vehicle cannot automatically take that duty.
For Innova and Crysta bookings, capture the passenger count and required passenger seats. A model match
never overrides insufficient capacity. A Crysta may fulfil an Innova booking; a normal Innova cannot fulfil a
Crysta booking.
Scope: apply these matching rules within the existing approval, dispatch-release timing and open-booking status rules. This is a
focused addendum to the earlier application specification.

02 / ROLE BEHAVIOUR & BOOKING CHANGES
Vendor flow & assignment
Keep seeing a duty, accepting a duty and assigning a vehicle as separate checks.
Stage
Required behaviour
Owner Driver: list / accept
Filter by the approved attached vehicle using the matrix on page 1, then apply capacity and
the existing eligibility checks. Recheck the current vehicle when the driver accepts.
Vendor: list
Show all five duty categories available to Vendors, even when the Vendor currently has no
vehicle matching a particular duty. Do not hide that duty because of the fleet composition.
Vendor: accept
Allow acceptance of any category of open duty under the existing claim rules. A missing
matching vehicle does not itself block the claim. Reserve the accepted duty to that Vendor.
Vendor: assign
Only allow an approved, available Driver and an approved, available Vehicle. The selected
vehicle must pass the page 1 matrix and the booking capacity check. Revalidate vehicle
swaps/reassignments.
No matching approved
vehicle
Keep the duty accepted but awaiting assignment. The Vendor may add a suitable vehicle
for Admin approval or contact Admin. Do not silently assign an incompatible vehicle.
Vendor employee Driver
Receives only the trip assigned to that driver. "Vendors see all categories" does not give
employee Drivers access to Open Duties.
Customer-approved alternative vehicle
1. Call centre / Admin checks the actual passenger count, required space and the available vehicle, then obtains
the customer's agreement to the alternative category/model.
2. Admin records the original and revised booking requirement, customer consent, reason, operator and time.
Any fare change must be agreed and recorded before assignment.
3. Re-run matching against the revised booking. For example, a seven-seat SUV booking for five passengers
may be changed to SUV 6+1 with consent. Seven passengers must not be placed in six passenger seats.
No direct bypass of the matching rules
Forwarding a duty is not permission to assign a smaller or different-category vehicle. Update the approved
booking requirement first, then apply the same matching and capacity checks.
Fare handling - recommended implementation
Show the booked category and agreed fare on the duty card. If an eligible SUV, Innova or Crysta takes a
Sedan booking, retain the agreed Sedan booking fare. A larger vehicle must not automatically trigger a
higher-category price.
The assignment deadline, escalation and any re-release of an unassigned Vendor claim continue to use the main specification /
configured Admin policy; this addendum does not invent a new timeout.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION & ACCEPTANCE
Developer checks
These implementation details support the confirmed matrix and Vendor rule. Validate them on the server as
well as in the app interface.
Minimum information to keep separate
Vehicle: ID, verified model/family, passenger seats excluding driver, approval and availability.
Booking: Category, passenger count, required passenger seats, agreed fare and approved version.
Assignment: Accepted partner, Driver ID, Vehicle ID and the booking version checked.
Matching: use an explicit category matrix, then verify seating capacity and existing eligibility. Do not infer
compatibility from a simple price ranking or the word "SUV" alone. Innova and Innova Crysta must remain
distinct model families.
