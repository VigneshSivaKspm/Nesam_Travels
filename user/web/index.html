Frequently Asked Questions

How does NESAM one-way drop taxi work in Tamil Nadu?

With NESAM one-way drop taxi, you only pay for the one-way distance between your pickup location and destination. There is no return fare charge, making it significantly more affordable than traditional round-trip rentals.

How do I cancel my booking?

Scheduled rides can be cancelled free of charge up to 1 hour before scheduled pickup time through the site or customer helpline.

What payment methods are accepted?

You pay your chauffeur at the end of the trip via Cash or UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm). Nothing is charged upfront during online booking.

How are tolls and permits charged?

Tolls, state entry permits, and parking are paid at actual cost against receipts, ensuring complete transparency with zero hidden markups.

What is the 4-digit boarding code?

A secure 4-digit code generated exclusively for your trip. Share it with your driver only once you are seated inside the car; the trip cannot start without it.
